| Check | Verify check `coverage`: `cargo xtask coverage` |

Verify check `coverage`: `cargo xtask coverage` runs `cargo llvm-cov --branch` (0.9.1) on the tooling nightly and requires 100 % of regions, functions, lines, and branches in `aldis-core`, `aldis-time`, `aldis-epp`, `aldis-spp`, and `aldis-aos` (with `--all-features`), less the waivers in `coverage-waivers.toml` (none; an oversized waiver also fails). Test modules are excluded with `coverage(off)`. `aldis-ffi` is measured and reported, not gated (`REPORTED` in `xtask/src/coverage.rs`, M2 plan D16). `cargo xtask consistency` fails when a Layer 0–2 crate that declares a function is not in `GATED`, or a gated crate is a skeleton.
