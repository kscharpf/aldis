The EPP and time targets check self-consistency, not an independent model, except the CUC bound; agreement with the Blue Book comes from the hand-derived vectors (and the WP-4 cross-check); `nightly.yml` job `fuzz` runs each for 600 s per night (`fuzz-seconds` = 3600 before a release) and opens an issue on a crash. Decoders and engines add their targets as they are implemented. `cargo xtask consistency` (part of `cargo xtask ci`) fails when a target of `file:fuzz/Cargo.toml` is missing from the `nightly.yml` fuzz matrix, or the other way round. The gating smoke check `fuzz-smoke` in `cargo xtask verify` (`cargo xtask fuzz smoke`) builds all fuzz targets and executes each for a smoke pass (`-runs=1 -max_total_time=2`, testing corpus seeds if present) to block stale assertions and harness breakages before merge (#124, #135).
