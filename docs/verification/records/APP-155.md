The lossy run: driver `--case a3-nominal --drop up:5,up:9,down:25,…,down:35` and `ops.py --shift DUMP_MEMORY=0.3` (§11.6).

**APP-164, `a3-nominal`: met.** Seven frames, N(S) 0 to 6, each accepted once, in order (`cmd_received` ×6 and `table_received` (1, 100)); the CLCW N(R) ran 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 (V(S) = 7 after the last command), inside the bounds of the criterion, with no Lockout and no Retransmit flag; YAMCS reports all seven commands as acknowledged (`Acknowledge_Sent` OK), the COP-1 state was `ACTIVE` in all 141 polls, the largest `txCount` was 1, and its log has no warning besides the security notice. The projection of the commands equals `a3-nominal`'s but for the `table_received` event, (1, 100) in place of (1, 300), and the packets that depend on it (21 of 58); `judge.py` prints "NOT met" on the line that compares with the golden trace for that reason, and "equal to a3-nominal's except the length of the table: True" under it, which is the criterion as amended.

**APP-165, the lossy-link case: met.** The same projection at the end (N(R) = 7 = V(S), no Lockout, `ACTIVE` throughout, no `cop1TxLimit` reached: largest `txCount` 2, no alert). The flight node discarded the frame N(S) = 5 behind the dropped N(S) = 4 (E3, `frame_discarded` (2, 3) at 5.060 s) and the CLCW of 5.125 s carried the Retransmit flag; YAMCS retransmitted both frames at 5.130 s (accepted, E1); the retransmitted `ACTIVATE_TABLE` at 4.036 s, its CLCWs having been lost, was discarded as a duplicate (E4, `frame_discarded` (2, 4)); the lone `SET_PAYLOAD_MODE` (on) dropped at 6.030 s was retransmitted by T1 at 7.030 s. The driver dropped uplink units 5 and 9 and downlink units 25 to 35, as scripted (`fault` records). YAMCS's one warning is "Incomplete packet dropped because of frame loss", for the dropped downlink frames.

**APP-156, the settings.** Those listed in §11.9 (SCID `0x155`; TC frames at most 128 octets with FECF; one VC; Segment Header `0xC0`; no CLTU, no marker; K = 4; T1 = 1 s in whole seconds; Transmission_Limit 3 as `cop1TxLimit`; Timeout_Type 0 set through the HTTP API, #561; the start *Initiate AD Service (without CLCW check)*); the configuration files are those of `refapp/interop/yamcs/` at the node commit above.
