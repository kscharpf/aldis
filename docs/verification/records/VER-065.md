The hazard and failure-mode analysis is kept in [hazards.md](../hazards.md) and, one file per frame codec, in [`hazards/`](../hazards/) (`tm.md`, `tc.md`, `aos.md`, `uslp.md`), written in M2 WP-8 (branch `m2/assurance`, prepared 2026-10-25 by Claude Code). Its format and its references are machine-checked by `cargo xtask assurance` (check `spec`); that check does not show that the analysis is complete or that a control does what the text says (assurance-evidence.md §6). That is this inspection.

Status: complete for M2. The review is part of the M2 exit (milestone plan `docs/milestones/m2-frame-codecs.md`, exit table, row "Hazards and assurance case"). At the exit the reviewer, who is not the author of the analysis:

- checks that every failure-mode category of hazards.md §1.2 appears in each codec file, or has the reason it does not apply, and reads each `n/a` reason against the Blue Book clause it cites;
- looks for failure modes that no category names (defeater DF-15 of the [assurance case](../assurance-case.md)) by reading the codec's requirements and Blue Book once more, and raises each as a proposal, not as a requirement or open issue the analysis adds itself;
- checks, for each hazard, that the controls named exist, that the evidence rows show what the text says, and that the residual risk and integrator obligations are stated;
- checks that each proposal of the analysis has a recorded disposition (accepted into a work package, an open issue, or declined by the role that decides), and that `cargo xtask assurance --strict` reports no problem;
- records the result below as a row "Reviewed <date>, <milestone>, by <reviewer>: ...", and the maintainer approves it with the milestone exit.

The same review is repeated at the exit of each later milestone for the standards it covers.
