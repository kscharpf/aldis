Inspected 2026-11-18 ([#634](https://github.com/kscharpf/aldis/issues/634), decision [#630](https://github.com/kscharpf/aldis/issues/630)). The crate documentation of `aldis-core` (`file:crates/aldis-core/src/lib.rs`, the module table and the section "Codec-facing tier") names the codec-facing tier: the modules `bits` (`file:crates/aldis-core/src/bits.rs`) and `container` (`file:crates/aldis-core/src/container.rs`), and the traits `FrameFormat` and `PhysicalChannel` (`file:crates/aldis-core/src/format.rs`), `FramePlan`, `FinishPlan`, and `VariablePlan` (`file:crates/aldis-core/src/writer.rs`), `Delimiter` and `StreamFrame` (`file:crates/aldis-core/src/stream.rs`). Each of the nine says so in its own docs and cites GEN-082. Every other public item is integrator-facing.

Classification of the modules. Integrator-facing by use in `refapp/` or by root re-export: `error`, `field`, `mode`, `anomaly`, `ids`, `time`, `crc`, `ocf`, `oid`, `counter`, `security`, `engine`. Integrator-facing because the signatures of the codecs return their types: `writer`, `finishing`, `checked`, `plain`, `stream`, `lookup`, `format`, `cop`, `layout`. In the last group the traits above are codec-facing and the other items of the module are not.

The line is held by `cargo xtask refapp-deps` (`file:xtask/src/refapp.rs`, verify check `layers`): no Rust source under `refapp/` names the two modules or the seven traits. Version: the Aldis crates take one version from `version.workspace` in `file:Cargo.toml` (0.7.0). They are `publish = false` and depend on each other by `path` with no `version` requirement, so no range exists and no exact-version pin is written yet; the pin belongs to the first publication.
