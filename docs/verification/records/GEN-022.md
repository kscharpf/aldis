Inspections of [`aldis-core`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-core.md), [`aldis-time`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-time.md), [`aldis-spp`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-spp.md), [`aldis-epp`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-epp.md), [`aldis-aos`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-aos.md) (the FHEC of M2 WP-4, prepared 2026-10-20 by Claude Code, approved by the maintainer 2026-10-21), [`aldis-tm`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-tm.md) (M2 WP-2, prepared 2026-10-23 by Claude Code, pending exit review), [`aldis-tc`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-tc.md) (M2 WP-3, prepared 2026-10-23 by Claude Code, pending exit review), and [`aldis-uslp`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-uslp.md) (M2 WP-5, prepared 2026-10-23 by Claude Code, pending exit review). Pre-release re-inspection for `v0.2.0-m1` at `main` 5f40e8b, prepared 2026-10-20 by Claude Code, approved by the maintainer 2026-10-20: no recursion; every loop is bounded by an input length, a const-generic capacity, or a constant; nothing blocks. Earlier inspections, each approved by the maintainer: `aldis-core` 2026-10-16 (revisions for `mode`/`anomaly` and review round 1 approved 2026-10-17, for issue #22 confirmed 2026-10-19); `aldis-epp` 2026-10-18 (revisions after the PR #18 review and for issue #22 confirmed); `aldis-time` prepared 2026-10-18, approved 2026-10-19 (revisions for the value types, issue #22, and the ASCII codes and conversions confirmed 2026-10-19); `aldis-spp` 2026-10-19 (revision for segmentation, reassembly, and the time secondary header confirmed 2026-10-19). From 2026-10-21 (VER-061, maintainer decision of that date), each change that adds a loop or a call revises the crate's record in the same pull request, prepared by its author and pending exit review, and the maintainer approves all revisions since the last approval once, at the milestone exit review or before a release. [`aldis-ffi`](../inspections/gen-022-023-aldis-ffi.md): partial, the `aldis-core` C ABI of M2 WP-1 and the shared pointer, handle, and status checks, prepared 2026-10-23 by Claude Code, pending exit review; the M1 codec modules of WP-0c follow in M2 WP-7.

Since 2026-10-24 (M2 WP-7, branch `m2/wp7-checks`), two checks mechanize part of GEN-022; the inspections remain the verification. `cargo xtask loops` (`xtask/src/loops.rs`; part of `cargo xtask ci` and the verify check `spec`) finds every loop in the library code of each crate (`for`, `while`, `loop`, the consuming iterator methods, and the slice methods that loop) and fails when the crate's record does not name it, by file, item, and index or label in the `ID` column of its Loops table, in a row with a bound, or when a record names a loop that does not exist; on 2026-10-24 it found 136 loops in nine crates, and seven loops of `aldis-ffi` (`lib.rs`, `spp_state.rs`, `time.rs`) that its record lacked, now added. The verify check `stack` (record [VER-011](VER-011.md)) builds the call graphs of the C ABI static library and of the exerciser from their object code and fails on a cycle, so no recursion reaches any entry point on the three bare-metal targets. The Kani harness `error_kind_lookup_is_bounded` (`crates/aldis-core/src/proofs.rs`, nightly check `kani`) proves the bound of `error.rs ErrorKind::from_code #1` for every code; the harnesses of [formal-proofs.md](../formal-proofs.md) §2 prove the bounds of the CRC and bit-field loops up to their input lengths.
