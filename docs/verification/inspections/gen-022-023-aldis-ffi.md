## Loops

Every loop in the inspected library code, with its bound. `crc.rs`, `ocf.rs`, `oid.rs`, `handle.rs`, and `error.rs` contain none of their own; `abi.rs` `configs` and `Configs::get` have none either (one element per call). Copies of fixed-size values (the four OCF octets, a handle's tag and state, `aldis_error_detail_t`) run over a constant length and are not listed. Loops inside `aldis-core` functions that these modules call are bounded in the [`aldis-core` record](gen-022-023-aldis-core.md) and listed here only by the bound they take from the call.

| Location | Loop | Bound |
|---|---|---|
| `abi.rs` `bytes_mut` | `any` over the call's input buffers, to reject an overlap | constant per call site: the length of the `inputs` array literal (1 for `aldis_oid_generator_fill` and `aldis_tm_oid_writer_begin`, the generator's storage; 5 for `aldis_tm_encode`, its field buffers; 0 for the other TM writers; 1 for `aldis_tc_encode`, its User Data; 0 for the TC writers and `aldis_tc_control_encode`) |
| `tm.rs` `master_channel` | `for i in 0..vcs.len()`: one `Configs::get`, `virtual_channel`, and `TmMasterChannelConfig::with_vc` per struct; the first error ends it | input length: `vc_count`, the caller's array (`abi::configs` checked that `vc_count` × stride octets form one range) |
| `tm.rs` decoders, through `TmFrame::decode`, `decode_with`, `decode_unchecked`, `validate` | the FECF check, and the constant-length comparisons of `validate` | input length: the frame (inspected in `aldis-tm` and `aldis-core`) |
| `tm.rs` writers, through `encode`, `TmFrameWriter::begin`, `resume`, `begin_oid`, `finish` | zero-fill, the field copies, the OID fill, the FECF | input length: the frame, at most `cap` (inspected in `aldis-tm` and `aldis-core`); `GeneratorState::Galois` fill in `aldis_tm_oid_writer_begin`: the data field × 8 bits |
| `tc.rs` `parse_all` (from `master_channel`, once for the VCs and once for the MAPs) | `[first; N]` initialisation; `for` over `items.iter_mut().enumerate().take(configs.len()).skip(1)`: one `Configs::get` and one `virtual_channel` or `map_channel` per struct; the first error ends it | compile-time capacity: `N` = `ALDIS_TC_MAX_VCS` (64) or `ALDIS_TC_MAX_MAPS` (`capacities.toml`); a longer array is rejected before the loop |
| `tc.rs` `master_channel`, through `VcTable::new` | the duplicate, MAP, and room checks of the codec | compile-time capacity: O((64 + `ALDIS_TC_MAX_MAPS`)²) (inspected in `aldis-tc`) |
| `tc.rs` decoders, through `TcFrame::decode`, `validate_frame`, and the `VcLookup` of `VcTable` | the FECF check; the channel lookups | input length: the frame; capacity: 64 + `ALDIS_TC_MAX_MAPS` (inspected in `aldis-tc` and `aldis-core`) |
| `tc.rs` writers, through `encode`, `TcFrameWriter::begin`, `resume`, `finish` | the User Data copy, the zeros of the Security Header and Trailer, the trailer move, the FECF | input length: the frame, at most `cap` (inspected in `aldis-tc` and `aldis-core`) |

| `oid.rs` `GeneratorState::fill`, through `OidGenerator::fill` or `OidGeneratorGalois::fill` | over the output octets, 8 bits each | output length (`len`) × 8 |

## Recursion

No function calls itself, directly or through another `aldis-ffi` function. The internal call graph of the inspected modules is acyclic; its longest chain has seven functions, through `tc.rs` (last item below), and six through `tm.rs`; outside them, five: `aldis_oid_generator_fill` → `abi::call` → (closure) `generator` → `handle::get` → `abi::invalid_argument`. The calls:

- `aldis_crc16` → `abi::call`; in its closure `abi::bytes` → `range`, `abi::out`, `Out::write`, and `aldis_core::crc::crc16`.
- `aldis_ocf_classify` → `abi::call`; in its closure `octets` → `abi::bytes`, `abi::invalid_argument`; `abi::out`, `Out::write`, `report`, and `Ocf::decode`.
- `aldis_ocf_check` → `abi::call`; in its closure `octets`, then one of the `TryFrom` constructors of `Type1Report`, `ProjectSpecificReport`, `FsrReport`, or `abi::invalid_argument`.
- `aldis_oid_generator_init` → `abi::call`; in its closure `abi::out`, `OidGenerator::new` or `OidGeneratorGalois::new` or `abi::invalid_argument`, `handle::init` → `abi::invalid_argument` or `Error::buffer_too_small`, and `Out::write`.
- `aldis_oid_generator_fill` → `abi::call`; in its closure `generator` → `handle::get`, `Handle::range`, `abi::bytes_mut` → `range`, `Handle::load`, `GeneratorState::fill`, and `Handle::store`.
- `aldis_oid_generator_deinit` → `handle::deinit` → `handle::get`.
- `aldis_oid_generator_storage_size` and `_storage_align` return a constant.
- `abi::call` → `write_detail` (straight-line: one guarded write per member), `aldis_error_detail_t::from` (one `match`), and `error::status`.
- `abi::configs` → `range`, `abi::invalid_argument`; `Configs::get` → `abi::invalid_argument`.
- `tm.rs`: every `aldis_tm_*` entry point → `abi::call`. In the closures: `aldis_tm_config_check`, `aldis_tm_peek_length`, `aldis_tm_decode`, and `aldis_tm_decode_unchecked` → `abi::config`, `physical` → `abi::flag`, `TmPhysicalChannelConfig::new`; the functions that take a master channel → `master_channel_at` → `abi::config`, `abi::configs`, `master_channel` → `physical`, `fsh_len`, `abi::flag`, `Configs::get`, `virtual_channel` → `fsh_len`, `abi::flag`, `abi::invalid_argument`, and the `aldis-tm` and `aldis-core` constructors; `abi::bytes`, `abi::mode`, `abi::out`, `Out::write`, and the `aldis-tm` decoder or writer; the decoders → `view` → `header`, `layout` → `span`; `aldis_tm_header_decode` → `header`; the writers → `fields` (→ `abi::flag`, `abi::invalid_argument`, `PacketOffset::try_from`) and `Fields::checked` (→ `TmMasterChannelConfig::vc`, `frame_layout`), or `oid_fields`, `abi::read`, `abi::bytes_mut`, `layout`; `aldis_tm_encode` → `abi::read` of the content, `aldis_tm_content_t::buffers`, `ocf`; `aldis_tm_validate` → `TmFrame::decode_with`, `TmFrame::validate`; `aldis_tm_oid_writer_begin` → `oid::generator` → `handle::get`, `Handle::range`, `Handle::load`, `Handle::store`. ## Blocking

There is no I/O, locking, atomics, sleep, or spin-wait. `std` is only behind a feature and in test modules; the `no_std` panic handlers (`lib.rs`) are never reached (VER-010). Every inspected function is O(input length) or O(1) by the table above; a TM call that takes a master channel is O(frame length + `vc_count`), and a TC call O(frame length + (64 + `ALDIS_TC_MAX_MAPS`)²).
