# aldis-link

Sans-I/O sending and receiving engines for CCSDS packets and frames: packet-to-frame generation, Master and Virtual Channel multiplexing, frame demultiplexing, packet extraction, and segmentation and reassembly.

## Where it sits

Layer 2 (engines) of the [architecture](../../docs/01-architecture.md) §2. It depends on `aldis-core`, and on each of `aldis-spp`, `aldis-epp`, `aldis-tm`, `aldis-tc`, `aldis-aos`, and `aldis-uslp` only with the feature of the same name. The packet engines (the `PacketGenerator`, the `PacketExtractor`, the `MapSegmenter`, and the `MapReassembler`) build with either of `spp` and `epp`, or both, and compile no code of the codec not selected (GEN-009): a packet of the absent PVN is refused at `SubmitPacket`, and an absent PVN or idle kind at configuration. The shared packet types (`ValidPvns`, `IdlePacketKind`) and `PacketProtocol` exist with `spp` or `epp`. The engines over USLP (`PacketGenerator<Uslp>`, `PacketExtractor<Uslp>`) need `epp`, because USLP fills a fixed-length data field with an Encapsulation Idle Packet (LNK-013, LNK-063). The `PacketGenerator` and the `PacketExtractor` (M4 WP-3) serve a protocol of `tm`, `aos`, or `uslp`; the `MapSegmenter` and the `MapReassembler` (M4 WP-6) serve `tc` or `uslp`, and USLP with `spp` alone. The `CltuPacker` (M4 WP-6) needs no codec. The `ChannelMux`, the `AccessGenerator`, and the `BitstreamGenerator` (M4 WP-4) need only the feature of the protocol they serve (`tm`, `aos`, `uslp`). The protocols `Tm`, `Tc`, `Aos`, and `Uslp` are the `FrameFormat` markers of the codecs and exist with the feature of the same name.
